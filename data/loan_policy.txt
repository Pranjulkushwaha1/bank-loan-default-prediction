Bank Loan Default Prediction Policy

1. Age Policy:
Applicants must be between 21 and 65 years of age.
Senior citizens above 60 may get special consideration.

2. Income Policy:
Minimum monthly income required is 15000 rupees.
Higher income reduces default risk significantly.

3. Debt Ratio Policy:
Debt ratio above 0.5 is considered high risk.
Ideal debt ratio should be below 0.3 for loan approval.

4. Risk Categories:
Low Risk (0-30%): Loan approved with standard rates.
Medium Risk (30-60%): Loan approved with higher interest.
High Risk (60%+): Loan rejected or requires guarantor.

5. Late Payment History:
More than 2 late payments in 90 days means high default risk.
Zero late payments indicates responsible borrower.
